Rung 1 · Re-Anchor & Pause
Trigger First Sign of Volume Spike
When to use: First Sign of Volume Spike
Escalation Point
Frontline Verbal Shape:

"I can hear how frustrated you are about this delay. I need us to lower our voices so we can solve it together."

Why This Works:

Acknowledges the underlying emotional driver without taking offense, while establishing the necessary working environment for problem-solving.

Frontline Rule:

Never tell a patient to 'calm down' (it sounds condescending). Instead, pair their distress with your practical operational requirement.
